## Installation

To install this swiplet in the app, go to `Swiplets - Add - Official Swiplets - Skip Chinese App Ads`. You can also download and install the swiplet package from [GitHub release](https://github.com/swipt-automation/official-swiplets/releases/tag/nightly).

## How It Works

We used `ipatool` and `unfairplay` to download, decrypt, and reverse engineer apps in bulk, adding support for the vast majority of popular apps on the App Store in mainland China.

We also identified a large number of Chinese ad SDKs. In apps that aren't supported yet, turning on `Fuzzy Skipping` can still recognize ads and skip them faster next time.

If an app doesn't work correctly or isn't supported yet, please [let us know](https://github.com/swipt-automation/official-swiplets/issues). You can also open a pull request directly on [official-swiplets](https://github.com/swipt-automation/official-swiplets).
