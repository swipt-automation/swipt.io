title: Switch App Store Account
---

`Switch App Store Account` is a script swiplet that operates your iPhone or iPad to switch App Store accounts.

## Installation

To install this swiplet in the app, go to `Swiplets - Add - Official Swiplets - Switch App Store Account`. You can also download and install the swiplet package from [GitHub release](https://github.com/swipt-automation/official-swiplets/releases/tag/nightly).

## Usage

In iOS 27, signing in to the App Store with your `Device iCloud Account` only requires a confirmation. So you only need to add the email addresses and passwords of your other accounts to this app (you can fill them in with `Passwords`) to switch between them.
